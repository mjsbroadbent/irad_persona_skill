# Maintenance files (not part of the installed skill)

Everything in this folder is for auditing and reverting changes. Claude should never read it for writing tasks, and it can be left out when installing the skill.

| Path | What it is |
|---|---|
| `CHANGELOG.md` | Dated record of every lore and wiki change since October 2026, newest first |
| `CHANGELOG_wiki_review.md` | Record of the 2026 wiki review, article by article |
| `archive/pre_review/` | Copies of all lore files and volumes from before the 2026 review |
| `superseded/baselore.md`, `superseded/newlore.md` | The two canon files that `canon.md` replaced on 2026-10-12 (newlore was a dated log of corrections; baselore the original synthesis) |
| `Irad_Wiki_Original.md` | The original Irad Wiki article that baselore was synthesized from |
