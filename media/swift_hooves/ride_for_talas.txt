Ride for Talas
New-traditionalist country (1993 Nashville radio single); male baritone lead; fiddle, pedal steel, twangy electric guitar; galloping train beat; gang-vocal chant; key change into the last chorus. Written by Gene Ashby and Tommy Lee Varner, sung by Clay Beaudry. 3:41.

[Intro]
(fiddle and pedal steel, galloping snare)

[Verse 1]
There's a light in the window of a town called Talas
Where the river runs cold off the mountain pass
In the spring of '92 came the iron and the smoke
Down the Chu road rollin' with the red flags broke
Then they cut off the water and the bread ran thin
And the old church bell called the horsemen in

[Pre-Chorus]
From the high summer pastures, from the canyon floor
Came a thousand saddles, and a thousand more

[Chorus]
So ride, ride for Talas
Ride through the dust and the flame
Ride, ride for Talas
Every rider knows her name
Hold the line till the morning
Hold the cross up to the sky
Ride, ride for Talas
Ride on, Irad, ride

[Verse 2]
He was sixteen years with his granddaddy's gun
And a grey Akyel mare that could outrun the sun
His mama tied a cross on the bridle strap
Said "Son, you ride for Talas, then you ride on back"
Seventy nights where the river bends
Wolves at the door and no help to send

[Pre-Chorus]
But they held at the bridges and they held at the square
And the old men sang the old songs there

[Chorus]
So ride, ride for Talas
Ride through the dust and the flame
Ride, ride for Talas
Every rider knows her name
Hold the line till the morning
Hold the cross up to the sky
Ride, ride for Talas
Ride on, Irad, ride

[Bridge]
(half-time, steel guitar swell)
Now some swore they saw a rider dressed in white
Ride along the line in the dead of night
Never fired a shot, never said a word
But every frightened heart on that line was heard

[Gang Vocal Chant]
Atka min! Atka min!
(mount up, mount up)
Atka min! Atka min!

[Fiddle Solo]

[Verse 3]
When the June sun rose on the eighteenth day
All the guns on the Chu road had rolled away
And the kids in Talas, they will tell you true
That the riders held the city till the dawn came through

[Key Change]

[Final Chorus]
So ride, ride for Talas
Ride through the dust and the flame
Ride, ride for Talas
Every rider knows her name
Hold the line till the morning
Hold the cross up to the sky
Ride, ride for Talas
Ride on, Irad, ride

[Outro]
Ride on, Irad
Ride on
(fiddle fades over hoofbeats)
