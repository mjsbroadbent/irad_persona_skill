Morkhan, Karañgy Ee ("Morkhan the Dark Lord")
tüntolk, Jolchu (1999). Dark synth with liturgical-chant melody; dark choir. Iradi text as rewritten 2026-10-13 (approved by the user).

[Verse]
Kara tün, jandar titireyt,            Black night, the souls tremble,
Morkhandyn küüsü jürök alat.          Morkhan's song takes the heart.
Karga chakyrat, kün batat,            The crow calls, the sun goes down,
Morkhandyn küüsü kechke tögülöt.      Morkhan's song pours into the evening.

[Chorus]
Morkhandyn küchü tüshüp kelet,        Morkhan's power comes down,
Turmush kenetten agyp ketet.          Life suddenly flows away.

[Verse]
Jash agat, jan chygat,                Tears flow, the soul departs,
Morkhandyn jaryn tappaysyñ.           You will not find the edge of Morkhan's abyss.
Jan bulutka uchup ketet,              The soul flies off into the clouds,
Kün ketti, karañgy basat.             The sun is gone, the darkness presses down.

[Chorus]
Morkhandyn küchü jarkyrap turat,      Morkhan's power stands shining,
Turmush kushtay bulutka uchat.        Life flies like a bird into the clouds.

[Verse]

Kara kün nurga karayt.                The black day looks toward the light.
Morkhandyn küchü, jürökter küyöt,     Morkhan's power, the hearts burn,
Kara jash, tüshkön kün.               Black tears, the fallen day.
