# Irad Quick Reference (public layer, Oct 2026)

Precedence: `newlore.md` > `baselore.md` > wiki. Nothing here comes from `hiddenlore.md`. For Toktarov's health, give the official line ("extended health retreat", since Jul 2024) and attribute deepfake or synthesis claims to opposition outlets and tech monitors. Naming: urban ISEZ names end in -ov/-ova; tribal names use *X uulu* / *X kyzy*.

## 1. Who's who

**Toktarov family and DPI**
- **Erlan Toktarov** (b. 12 Apr 1954): Governor of the ISEZ and DPI chairman since 2007. Ex-Soviet logistics officer; Senate Majority Leader 2000–04. Proclaimed the ISEZ on 25 Feb 2007 after leaking Orozbekov's secret partition deal (the "Bill of Sale"), which made him a national hero. Unseen since Jul 2024.
- **Anara Toktarova** (b. 1956): his wife; children's charity.
- **Ruslan Toktarov** (b. 22 Jun 1978): eldest son. Major general commanding the Gendarmerie since Mar 2012 (Omsk tank school). Nationalist; Dec 2024 "guests" remark.
- **Alikhan Toktarov** (b. 14 Sep 1980): second son. IradOne chairman since 2015; Steppe Luxury Group. Bishkek and Regent's College; Western-leaning "playboy".
- **Timur** (b. 1982; Trans-Chuy), **Adil** (b. 1984; Talas Commercial Development Group, Tulpar FC) and **Bekzat** (b. 1986; NAI): low-profile business sons.
- **Zara Toktarova** (b. 14 Nov 1988): only daughter. Stanford education doctoral candidate; married Maksat 2008; seen as non-political.
- **Aidar Maksat** (b. 3 Nov 1980, Kobozev; born Maksatov): Lieutenant Governor since 25 Nov 2019; civil engineer (LSE). Runs the government day to day.
- **Mirlan Toktarov** (b. 1983): nephew; heads the Akyel Syndicate. **Daniyar Tologonov** (b. 1950): Lieutenant Governor 2007–19. **Nurbek Sultanov**: Mejlis Speaker since 2019.

**ISEZ officials and the territorial era**
- **Nurlanbek Ermatov** (b. 1947): territorial Governor 1995–2003. **Nurgul Abdrakhmanova** (b. 1955): Governor 2003–06, the first woman in the post.
- **Dastan Isakov** (b. 1946): Chief Justice 1999–2007; Statutory Independence Ruling.
- **Bakyt Orozbekov** (b. 19 Jul 1962): only DRI Prime Minister (9 Dec 2006 – 4 Jun 2007); secretly negotiated the *Satuu Kagazy* ("Bill of Sale") to partition Irad among its neighbours, leaked by Toktarov 25 Feb 2007; charged in absentia with treason 2008; in Istanbul.
- **Azamat Temirov** (b. 1969): Bank of Irad chairman since 2010. **Chingiz Asanov** (b. 1963): Minister of Natural Resources. **Samat Kasymov** (b. 1968): Attelkom Director-General.
- **Ruslan Kadyrov** (b. 1971): Gendarmerie major who defected to the Front in 2007. **Andrés Ochoa Kaparov** (b. 2000): first Mexican-Iradi councillor (2023).
- US High Commissioners Ashby, Whitaker, Lindqvist and Garrity (1992–2006).

**ATR, IAC and clans**
- **Bektur Zhumabek uulu** (b. 15 Aug 1968): Tanıp *biy*; led the Popular Front and the storming of Talas (4 Jun 2007); IAC spokesman (*elchi*); led the 2011 walkout.
- **Klara Zhumabek kyzy** (b. 1972): his sister; felt cooperatives. **Nurbolot Bektur uulu** (b. 1990): his son; Tanıp El-Choro captain.
- **Sabyrbek Duishön uulu** (b. 1966): Boztorgoy *biy*, the steppe rival. **Biybolot Moldosh uulu** (b. 1944): Karakoyun aksakal and mediator.
- **Döölötbek Ashym uulu** (b. 1957): Chubar *biy*. **Tabaldy Sultanaly uulu** (b. 1949): Kökjal elder.
- **Kubanychbek Toktobolot uulu**: Chancellery secretary since 2016. **Kanatbek Niyaz uulu** (b. 1950): Chatkal *adat* judge.
- **Kuban Mamyt uulu** (Mamytov, 1949–2013): "Ghost of Chatkal", 1992 irregulars.

**Opposition and media**

## 2. Timeline
- 3700–3100 BCE: Botai (horse-domestication claim). 104–101 BCE: War of the Heavenly Horses. 9th c.: Rabat-i-Safed. Jul 751: Battle of Talas.
- 11th c.: Janyl tradition; Old Iyık Janyl. 12th c.: first records of the Iradis (Qara Khitai). 1218–21: Mongol conquest. 1339–42: Ali-Sultan's persecution; Timur destroys the dioceses.
- **1727**: Kokand charter and Church of St. Janyl (consecrated 1730). 1820s: Talas citadel. 1864: Chernyayev takes the Talas forts. **Feb 1876**: Kokand annexed.
- **1916**: labor decree (25 Jun OS); revolt Aug–Oct; the Urkun flight (remembered 15 Aug). 1917–18: Iradi National Council.
- **1925**: ISSR. 1927: Kobozev founded. 1928: Latin alphabet. **14 Sep 1931**: *Orientalis Stella*.
- **1937–38**: NKVD clerical purges; Koryo-saram arrive. 1941: Cyrillic. 1944: Mobilization Strike. 1949: oil strike. 1952: air base built.
- **1954**: Virgin Lands campaign (beef and maize). **14–18 Jun 1956**: Talas riots (widow's horse shot). 1958: TSU founded. 1961: SBAW. 1962–mid-70s: Toktogul.
- 1968: *Zarina*. 1979: *Desert Sun's Rebellion*, *Rise of Glory*. 1986: Chernov First Secretary. 1989: Kobozev bread riots.
- **9 Oct 1991**: SRI. Dec 1991: Winter Uprising. Feb 1992: March of the Thousand Saddles. **11 Mar 1992**: Republic proclaimed (Irad Day).
- **10 Apr – 18 Jun 1992**: siege of Talas. **2 Jun**: Alimbekov–Baker Treaty. 6 Jun: Starlight landings. 12 Aug: Kobozev falls. **18 Aug**: SRI surrenders; US territory (c. 14,000 dead).
- Sep 1992: Nurzhan named. Oct: interim legislature; DPI founded. Nov: Supreme Court. Dec: Elective Governor Acts.
- 1993: Talasbank, Bank of Irad, som at 5/USD, NurGaz, *Talas Herald*, Labor Party, *Swift Hooves*. 1994: first elections; death penalty abolished; 3 Feb, *The Simpsons* "The Steppe Family Robinson" (pulled from US reruns 1995, shown yearly on IradOne). 1995: Ermatov; Jer-Suu.
- 1997: Criminal Code. 1998: TAEZ. 1999: SuKarne; Attelkom privatized. 2000: Northern Export Feeder. 2001: Basing Rights Agreement. 2004: UNESCO listing.
- **3 Dec 2006**: US withdrawal. 7 Dec: independence ruling. **9 Dec**: DRI proclaimed (26 recognitions).
- **25 Feb 2007**: ISEZ proclaimed in Nurzhan. Mar: Popular Front formed. **4 Jun**: Fall/Storming of Talas. **Sep**: Bishkek Ceasefire. 18 Oct: UNMI. Late 2007: IAC.
- 2008: referendum initiative; Jer-Suu banned. 2009: UN observers killed at Kenkol. **2010**: Parties Act. 2011: Kara-Oi talks fail; Labor dissolved; Toktarov 88.74%.
- **Jun 2013**: term limit removed. 2014: NurGaz Stadium riot. 2015: 78.25%, rivals struck off. 2017: Abduction Act. 2018: census (4.626M; true c. 6.2M); Uzbek border clashes.
- 2019: 90.31%; Maksat Lieutenant Governor. Aug 2021: Taliban alert. Aug 2022: Long Kurultai. Early 2023: move to Boz-Korgon (reported). **Oct 2023**: student protests. Nov 2023: 92.75%.
- **Jul 2024**: Governor's last appearance. **Feb 2025**: Trump "sale" remarks and protests. Jun 2026: incapacity petition. **2 Nov 2027**: election (term begins 22 Nov).

## 3. Places
- **Talas**: *de jure* capital, 1.12M. Old City (*Köhne Diz*), Bazar-i Kalan, Altyn Tower, Silk Road Arena, State Hippodrome.
- **Nurzhan** (Kobozev 1927–92): oil city, 850k. NurGaz, Refinery No. 1; Dastgoh and Navbahor districts.
- **Iyık Janyl**: holy city, 310k, at 1,280 m in the Fergana Range foothills, 20 km NE of Tash-Kömür (235 km by road from Talas).
- **Boz-Korgon**: compound 40 km east of Nurzhan (Chu District); family residence since 2011.
- **ISEZ districts**: Talas (air base, Kara-Bulak, UNMI HQ at **Kara-Oi**), Kara-Buura (**Kyzyl-Adyr**), Chu, Sary-Bulak (antimony), Lower Naryn (**Tash-Kömür**).
- **Semiz** (Otkormochny; *la Colonia*; *Meksikanka*): Mexican district, 23k. **Alatau Heights** (*Amerikanka*): returnee suburb. **Talas Air Base**: 12 km west of Talas.
- **ATR zones**: Northern Steppe, Karatau, Talas Alatau, Chatkal Highlands, Toktogul–Naryn, Fergana Rim.
- **Aksay Plateau**: Kurultai *zhayloo*. **Kara-Koyun Valley**. **Toktogul** (dam). **Ozgörüsh**. **Bazar-Korgon**. **Arslanbob**. **Mailuu-Suu** (uranium tailings).
- **Qara-Chik line**: 420 km. **Fergana Gateway** (2,480 m). **Kenkol Pass**. **Kok-Tash Kariz**. **Shar-i-Kuhna**. **Rabat-i-Safed**. **Kara-Suu Canyon**. **Besh-Tash National Park**. **Maryam-Bulak**. **Mazar-Too**. **Arsha-Say**.
- Renamings: Kobozev → Nurzhan; Otkormochny → Semiz; Iradneft → NurGaz; Kobozev Polytechnic → Nurzhan Polytechnic; Neftyanik Kobozev → Neftchi Nurzhan; Sary-Tash/Kadamjay → Sary-Bulak/Chatkal; Tash-Kumyr → Tash-Kömür.

- **DPI**: 1992; the "New DPI" after 2007; 51 of 60 Mejlis seats. **IAC**: late 2007; Kurultai, Chancellery, seven-judge Grazing Tribunal.
- **El-Choro**: c. 15,000 riders. **Popular Front** (2007).

- **Republican Gendarmerie** (*jandarm*): c. 4,000; named 1992, statutory 1993. **State Defense Corps** (MKK): Jul 2007; c. 12,000.
- **UNMI** (Oct 2007, Kara-Oi). **Department of Public Information**: government press office.
- **Syro-Iradi Catholic Church**: archeparchy plus Talas, Nurzhan and Chatkal eparchies; Synod; Order of St. Janyl the Hermit; St. Luka Mission.
- **IradOne** (1993; *IradOdin*; house programs *OneNews*, *OneDebate*, *OneTruth*, *OneBreakfast*). **Talas Herald** (1993; *Jarchy* 2017). **Irad Times** (1996). **FreeIrad.org**.
- **NurGaz** (1993). **Northern Export Feeder** (85 km, 2000). **Attelkom** (1994, privatized 1999). **Talasbank** (1993). **Bank of Irad** (som, 5/USD).
- **SuKarne complex** (1999) and **Conagra**, in the TAEZ (1998). **NAI**. **Trans-Chuy**. **Iradiya** (1997). **Akyel Syndicate** (1995). **SBAW** (1961).
- **Talasfilm** (1954). **Irad Film Society** (biennial festival). **Irad Palace of Art**. **TSU** (1958). **Altyn-Zhylky** (every December). **FFI** (1993).

- **Laws**: Organic Act (1992); Elective Governor Acts (1992; amended 2013); Banking and Currency Act (1993); State Symbols Act (1993); Holidays Act (1993/2008); Toponymic Heritage Statute (1993); Domestic Relations Act (1996); horse-slaughter ban (1996); Criminal Code (1997); Judicial Reforms Act (2003); Extremist Activity Act (2008); Border Protocol (2008); Political Parties Act (2010); Abduction for Marriage Act (2017).

## 5. Glossary
- **Irad**: Polaris. **zhulduz eli**: "people of the star". **kuble**: orientation toward Polaris. **ırda**: to sing; horseback song. **ırchy**: bard.
- **salyba**: Iradi cross (beads, lotus, no corpus). **kümöch**: unleavened bread. **malka** / **ene achytky**: holy leaven. **arsha**: juniper incense.
- **kashysh**: priest. **idta**: yurt chapel. **savma**: fast. **Salyba Künü**: 14 Sep. **Achytky Beyshembi**: Leaven Thursday. **at künü**: name day.
- **Morkhan**: bull-headed god, identified with Satan. **bakshy**: shaman. **tabyp**: healer. **tulpar**: winged horse. **albasty**: demon.
- **zhayloo** / **jayloo**: summer pasture. **kyshtoo**: winter pasture. **kymyz**: fermented mare's milk. **jamgyr tilöö**: rain rite.
- **uruk**: clan. **biy**: chief or judge. **aksakal**: elder. **adat**: custom law. **kun**: restitution. **toguz**: ninefold fine. **sanjyra**: genealogy.
- **Kurultai**: assembly. **tör**: seat of honor. **kamchy**: whip (the veto). **chetke turuu**: standing aside. **katchı**: secretary.
- **bata**: blessing (*oomin*). **koshok**: lament. **aytysh**: poetic duel. **küü**: komuz piece. **komuz** / **kyl-kyak**: lutes.
- **kuda**: in-laws. **kalym**: bride price. **sep**: dowry. **burakha**: crowning. **toi**: feast. **elechek**: married woman's headdress. **ala kachuu**: kidnapping.
- **zhylky** / **at**: horses / a horse. **körktüülük**: grace. **doran**: metallic sheen. **ulak tartysh** / **tai-kazan**: goat game / its goal. **kyz kuu**. **berkutchi**: eagle hunter.
- **ak-örgöö**: white yurt. **tündük**: yurt crown. **shyrdak**: felt rug. **ak-kalpak**: felt hat.
- **beshbarmak**. **kazy** (beef or lamb). **beet suzma**. **Jasil**. **Koryoin laghman**. **Irad paloosu** / *özbek paloo*.
- **Russos**. **Russocha**: code-switching. **chala-Irad**: "half-Iradi". **tilge kaytuu**: language revival. **kaytkandar**: returnees. **soodager**: smuggler.
- **Semiz** / **la Colonia** / **Meksikanka**. **Amerikanka**. **jandarm** (pejorative). **müchöl**: animal cycle. **arkar jyly**: stands in for the Cow year. **navbahor**: spring.
