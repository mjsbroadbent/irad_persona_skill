## 1. Who's who

- **1925**: ISSR. 1927: Kobozev founded. 1928: Latin alphabet. **14 Sep 1931**: *Orientalis Stella*.

- **1954**: Virgin Lands campaign (beef and maize). **14–18 Jun 1956**: Talas riots (widow's horse shot). 1958: TSU founded. 1961: SBAW. 1962–mid-70s: Toktogul.
- 1968: *Zarina*. 1979: *Desert Sun's Rebellion*, *Rise of Glory*. 1986: Chernov First Secretary. 1989: Kobozev bread riots.

## 3. Places
- **Talas**: *de jure* capital, 1.12M. Old City (*Köhne Diz*: mahalla ~1–1.6 km NW–N of the central square, citadel on its west side, Temir-Kapka N gate, Kalan Darwaza S gate, Bazar-i Kalan on its east side by the Kök-Bel road; Shahr-i Kabud garden quarter north of it), Soviet Centre east of the square, Trans-Talas Highway just south of it; Altyn Tower, Silk Road Arena, State Hippodrome. ~250 km² built up on both banks; railway station (branch terminus) ~1.4 km S of the central square, line along the south side.
- **Nurzhan** (Kobozev 1927–92): oil city, 850k. NurGaz, Refinery No. 1; Dastgoh and Navbahor districts. ~280 km² built up on both banks of the Chu; station on the through line a few hundred metres SW of the centre, freight depot ~4 km NE on the north line.
- **Iyık Janyl**: holy city, 310k, at ~650 m on the Naryn river flats where the river leaves its gorge, ~8 km upriver from Tash-Kömür, on State Route 1 (~347 km by road from Talas); old town and cathedral on **Cathedral Hill** (~800 m) above the east bank, reached by the ~4-km Cathedral Hill road from SR1, which runs through the city on the east bank; about two-thirds of the city (old bazaar centre, Lower Town, most housing) on the broad western flats; four road bridges over the Naryn; ~85 km² built up. **Diz-i Safid** (Kara-Khanid citadel, park) on a ~140-m limestone knoll in the middle of the city; **Shar-i-Kuhna** ruins ~2 km SW of the centre on the west bank.
- **Boz-Korgon**: compound 40 km east of Nurzhan (Chu District); family residence since 2011. Walled ~116 ha on a spur crest (~890 m, plain ~800 m) inside a fenced ~40 km² restricted area between two ravines, plus a corridor along the branch road and the eastern railway (no exclusion circle).
- **ISEZ districts** (six since 2016; three cities separate): Chu (~13,680 km², Nurzhan), Kara-Buura (~9,180; **Kyzyl-Adyr**; Qara-Chik village and checkpoint at the Kara-Buura valley mouth; also the plain north of the Kyrgyz Range east to the Merke stream), Talas (~2,700; air base, Kara-Bulak, Ming-Örük, UNMI HQ at **Kara-Oi**, Orto-Ayyl; up to the Kyrgyz Range crest), Lower Naryn (~2,260; **Tash-Kömür**; Kara-Köl below the dam, a narrow corridor down the Naryn gorge to the Uch-Kurgan dam at its mouth, the Iyık Janyl and Tash-Kömür flats, and the Fergana rim farm belt past **Bazar-Korgon** to the Uzbek frontier, ~145 km of it; the Uzbek piedmont towns across it, Karasu, Sultanabad and the rest, are not Irad; at the east end Suzak, Dostuk and the west-bank villages up the frontier river north of Jalal-Abad to below the Kök-Kamar Gorge, served by the Kök-Kamar road, ~33 km, 2010s; the valley villages east of the river and Kümüsh-Aziz, Munduz and Jiyde are Kyrgyz), **Toktogul** (~1,270; seat Toktogul; split from Lower Naryn by the 2016 district reform: the SR1 corridor from the Otmek tunnel over the Ala-Bel, the Toktogul basin floor with the town, reservoir and dam), Sary-Bulak (~470; antimony; Ak-Bulak, Uuk-Bulak, Tash-Bulak). ISEZ ~29,600 km² (~1/7 of ~217,400 km²); ATR ~187,800 km² (~6/7). Jalal-Abad city is in Kyrgyzstan.
- **Aulie-Ata** (Kara-Buura District, ~14k): old Kokand fort and Russian *uezd* town on the Talas ~35 km NNW of Kyzyl-Adyr; Soviet industrial city (~130k in 1989) emptied after 1992 and 2007 and cleared back to its old core; lowland railway junction ~3 km SE; gives its name to the Aulie-Ata cattle. Never renamed.
- **Diaspora:** biggest regional communities **Bishkek** and **Almaty**; Russia (70–120k seasonal); US 60–90k (Chicago). Iradis who move to **Uzbekistan** are mostly Muslim and often stop identifying as Iradi.
- **Semiz** (Otkormochny; *la Colonia*; *Meksikanka*): Mexican district, 23k. **Alatau Heights** (*Amerikanka*): returnee suburb. **Talas Air Base**: 12 km west of Talas.
- **ATR zones** (seven UN planning zones, none spanning ISEZ land): Northern Steppe (~119,400 km²; Betpak-Dala, Moiynkum, plain north of the ISEZ belt), Karatau (~24,000), Talas Alatau (~8,200; north slope and crest above the Talas valley only), Chatkal Highlands (~17,800; south of the Talas Alatau crest, west of the SR1 corridor and the Naryn gorge, incl. Uzun-Akmat with Ozgörüsh, Boz-Adyr, Kara-Jygach), Kyrgyz Range–Suusamyr (~4,100; Kyrgyz Range east of the Kök-Bel road, Suusamyr side east of SR1: Toguz-Bulak, Otmek, Karakol, Ak-Kyya), Toktogul–Naryn (~5,100; east of SR1 round the basin and east of the gorge; Kökjal), Fergana Rim (~9,200; the hills above the farm belt: Mailuu-Suu, Arslanbob, Kara-Unkur valley).
- **Aksay Plateau**: Kurultai *zhayloo*. **Kara-Koyun Valley** (Karakoyun Pass road to the Chatkal). **Toktogul** (dam and town, ISEZ since 2009; seat of Toktogul District since 2016). **Ozgörüsh**. **Bazar-Korgon** (ISEZ; Chubar lowland centre, the ISEZ's main Muslim town, border market). **Arslanbob** (tribal; Arslanbob-Ata shrine, Chubar highland seat). **Mailuu-Suu** (uranium tailings).
- **Qara-Chik line**: ~1,860 km; eight crossing points (Qara-Chik main, ~1,220 m at the Kara-Buura valley mouth ~15 km S of Kyzyl-Adyr, western sector, the only road to the Chatkal valley via the Karakoyun Pass; Aksay, Toguz-Bulak ×2, Kara-Bulak, Suusamyr, and the minor Mailuu-Suu road and Arslanbob road crossings) plus four internal control posts (Kara-Suu Canyon mouth, Otmek valley, Fergana Gateway, Naryn bridge); 12-km restricted zone. **Fergana Gateway** (Ala-Bel pass, 3,184 m, highest point of State Route 1; Talas–Tash-Kömür ~356 km via the ~5-km Otmek tunnel). Trans-Talas Highway ~194 km (Talas North Terminal on the Kazakh frontier, Kyzyl-Adyr km 135, Talas km 194); Talas–Nurzhan ~335 km (Kök-Bel road ~215 km, pass ~2,670 m on tribal land); railway ~690 km (Chu line ~229, Kara-Buura ~145 to the frontier, Talas branch ~100, north line ~129 east to the Kazakh frontier towards Otar, Boz-Korgon line ~42, spurs; the Shu–Mointy line north across the steppe is disused). **Kenkol Pass** (road ~31 km from Kara-Bulak up the Kenkol valley). **Kok-Tash Kariz**. **Shar-i-Kuhna**. **Rabat-i-Safed**. **Kara-Suu Canyon**. **Besh-Tash National Park**. **Maryam-Bulak**. **Mazar-Too**. **Arsha-Say**.
- Renamings: Kobozev → Nurzhan; Otkormochny → Semiz; Iradneft → NurGaz; Kobozev Polytechnic → Nurzhan Polytechnic; Neftyanik Kobozev → Neftchi Nurzhan; Sary-Tash/Kadamjay → Sary-Bulak/Chatkal; Tash-Kumyr → Tash-Kömür.

## 5. Glossary
- **Irad**: Polaris. **zhulduz eli**: "people of the star". **kuble**: orientation toward Polaris. **ırda**: to sing; horseback song. **ırchy**: bard.
- **salyba**: Iradi cross (patonce, rising from a lotus's central petal, no corpus). **kümöch**: unleavened bread. **malka** / **ene achytky**: holy leaven. **arsha**: juniper incense.
- **kashysh**: priest. **idta**: yurt chapel. **savma**: fast. **Salyba Künü**: 14 Sep. **Achytky Beyshembi**: Leaven Thursday. **at künü**: name day.
- **Morkhan**: bull-headed god, identified with Satan. **bakshy**: shaman. **tabyp**: healer. **tulpar**: winged horse. **albasty**: demon.
- **zhayloo** / **jayloo**: summer pasture. **kyshtoo**: winter pasture. **kymyz**: fermented mare's milk. **jamgyr tilöö**: rain rite.
- **uruk**: clan. **biy**: chief or judge. **aksakal**: elder. **adat**: custom law. **kun**: restitution. **toguz**: ninefold fine. **sanjyra**: genealogy.
- **Kurultai**: assembly. **tör**: seat of honor. **kamchy**: whip (the veto). **chetke turuu**: standing aside. **katchı**: secretary.
- **bata**: blessing (*oomin*). **koshok**: lament. **aytysh**: poetic duel. **küü**: komuz piece. **komuz** / **kyl-kyak**: lutes.
- **kuda**: in-laws. **kalym**: bride price. **sep**: dowry. **burakha**: crowning. **toi**: feast. **elechek**: married woman's headdress. **ala kachuu**: kidnapping.
- **zhylky** / **at**: horses / a horse. **körktüülük**: grace. **doran**: metallic sheen. **ulak tartysh** / **tai-kazan**: goat game / its goal. **kyz kuu**. **berkutchi**: eagle hunter. **köch**: a herding family's seasonal move.
- **ak-örgöö**: white yurt. **tündük**: yurt crown. **shyrdak**: felt rug. **ak-kalpak**: felt hat.
- **beshbarmak**. **kazy** (beef or lamb). **beet suzma**. **Jasil**. **Koryoin laghman**. **Irad paloosu** / *özbek paloo*.
- **Russos**. **Russocha**: code-switching. **chala-Irad**: "half-Iradi". **tilge kaytuu**: language revival. **kaytkandar**: returnees. **soodager**: smuggler.
- **Semiz** / **la Colonia** / **Meksikanka**. **Amerikanka**. **jandarm** (pejorative). **müchöl**: animal cycle. **arkar jyly**: grandmothers' name for the Cow year. **navbahor**: spring.
