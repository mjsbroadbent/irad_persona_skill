- Forum rules apply in-world: no real names of private people, no phone numbers, addresses
  or documents. Public figures of Irad (Toktarov family, Maksat, Bekzatov, Ilyasov, etc.)
  are fair game as rumour and opinion. Real-world public figures (Trump, Putin) only as

- New invented lore must be MINOR (a meetup, a café, a football score, a nickname). Grep
  canon.md/quickref.md/archive.json for any proper name you invent to avoid clashes. Never
  name a real living person. No links except to freeirad.org pages (e.g.
  https://freeirad.org/google.html), attelkom.net/users/... ring pages that exist in
  /home/claude/webring_sites, and iradone.com. No talasherald.com or irad.wiki links.
- Voice: real people. Typos and ESL English for some, lowercase kids, caps-lock truthers,
  long earnest diaspora posts, one-liners, "+1", "this", quotes of earlier posts,
  derails, in-jokes that build over years. Mostly short posts (1–5 sentences), some long.
  Use BBCode only: [b] [i] [u] [s] [quote=Name]...[/quote] [spoiler] [url=...]...[/url].
- Times are Irad Time (IRT, UTC+6), format "YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM". Posts in a thread must be
  in chronological order. A user can't post before their join date. Banned users can't
  post after their ban. Threads can span years (bumps). Vary times of day; Iradis post at
  night a lot; diaspora posts land at odd IRT hours.
